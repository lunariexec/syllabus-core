Basic 1 English Language, Unit 1. Learner's book with teacher's notes.

Lesson 1: Naming things around us

Sample indicator: Talk about things they see around them using simple words.

Lesson overview

Learners walk around the school compound, look closely and name the things they see. They practise saying simple sentences such as “I see a mango tree.”

Teacher's script

“Good morning, class. Today we are going on a nature walk around our school. Look carefully and listen well. When I clap my hands, stop and tell your partner one thing you can see.”

Activities

  1. Nature walk. Learners name three things they see: a mango tree, a palm tree, grass, stones, a hen or the clouds.
  2. Picture talk. In pairs, learners point to a picture card and say, “This is a goat.”
  3. Grow or not grow? Learners sort picture cards into two groups: things that grow and things that do not grow.

Support and extension

Extra support: use picture cards with the word printed underneath.
Extension: learners draw their favourite thing from the walk and say one sentence about it.

Check for understanding

  1. Which one is a tree? (a) goat (b) mango tree (c) stone
  2. Which one can fly? (a) bird (b) grass (c) rock
  3. What do we see in the sky? (a) clouds (b) goats (c) chairs
  4. Which one grows? (a) stone (b) palm tree (c) bucket
  5. Finish the sentence: “I see a …” (a) hen (b) run (c) big
